LITTLE ELSEWHERE / VISUAL RESEARCH

Living Text Art Library

Fourteen animated experiments in character-cell landscapes, visual illusions, and expressive typography. Every example is generated live, without image assets. Tap Pause to study the frames, or Copy recipe to reuse its parameters.

Design principle: Use seamless colored cells as the ground, animated characters as detail, and deliberate anomalies as clues. A world can feel alive without conventional sprites. Historical inspirations include ANSI/BBS art, PETSCII, ASCII demoscene effects, and roguelike display systems.
